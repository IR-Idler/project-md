Project MD
by Brighter World Labs

Brighter World Labs builds tools that help you be your best self, because change starts with you.

Project MD is free on Mac, iPhone and iPad, with more systems on the way. Every board is a plain Markdown file, so keep your boards in the cloud service of your choice (iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive…) and your projects stay in sync on all your devices.
